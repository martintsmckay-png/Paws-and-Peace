# Volunteer Safety Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**System:** Paws & Peace Core

## Purpose

Protect volunteers during animal care tasks.

## Guidelines

### Personal Safety

- Wear gloves when handling animals.
- Avoid entering yards without permission.
- Do not approach aggressive dogs alone.

### Environmental Safety

- Watch for ice, broken glass, or debris.
- Wear proper footwear.
- Carry a flashlight at night.

### Communication

- Check in with a coordinator before and after tasks.
- Report hazards immediately.
- Work in pairs when possible.
