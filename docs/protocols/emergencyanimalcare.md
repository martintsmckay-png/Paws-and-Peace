# Emergency Animal Care Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**System:** Paws & Peace Core

## Purpose

Provide immediate guidance for responding to injured or distressed animals.

## Roles Involved

- Safety
- Observer
- Coordinator

## Steps

### 1. Assess the Situation

- Determine if the animal is conscious.
- Check for bleeding, fractures, or breathing issues.
- Ensure the area is safe.

### 2. Stabilize

- Apply pressure to bleeding wounds.
- Keep the animal warm.
- Avoid unnecessary movement.

### 3. Contact Support

- Notify the coordinator.
- Prepare for mobile vet arrival.
- Provide location and condition details.

### 4. Transport (If Safe)

- Use blankets or makeshift stretchers.
- Avoid lifting alone.
- Keep the animal calm.

## Safety Notes

- Do not approach aggressive animals.
- Avoid mouth‑to‑mouth contact.
- Use gloves when possible.
