# Mobile Vet Services Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**System:** Paws & Peace Core

## Purpose

Provide a clear workflow for coordinating mobile veterinary services in remote communities.

## Roles Involved

- Coordinator
- Observer
- Caretaker

## Required Items

- Field notebook or phone
- Animal condition checklist
- Vaccination or treatment supplies (if applicable)

## Workflow

### 1. Pre‑Visit Preparation

- Confirm visit schedule with the vet team.
- Identify priority animals or households.
- Prepare data sheets or digital forms.

### 2. On‑Site Assessment

- Record animal condition.
- Note injuries, symptoms, or concerns.
- Take photos if permitted.

### 3. Treatment Support

- Assist with restraint if needed.
- Provide clean workspace.
- Record treatments administered.

### 4. Data Sync

- Upload or transcribe notes.
- Update central log.
- Flag follow‑up cases.

## Safety Notes

- Avoid handling aggressive animals alone.
- Use gloves when dealing with wounds.
- Report hazards immediately.
