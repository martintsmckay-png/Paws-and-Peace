# Community Stewardship Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**System:** Paws & Peace Core

## Purpose

Support community‑wide animal care through simple, repeatable tasks.

## Roles Involved

- Caretaker
- Observer
- Coordinator

## Core Responsibilities

- Monitor animal well‑being.
- Maintain clean water and food access.
- Report concerns early.

## Daily Tasks

- Refill water bowls.
- Check dog houses and shelters.
- Remove hazards (glass, wires, debris).

## Weekly Tasks

- Inspect shelters for damage.
- Refill straw or bedding.
- Check for roaming or injured animals.

## Reporting

- Log completed tasks.
- Note any issues requiring vet attention.
- Communicate with coordinators.
