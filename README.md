### 🛡️ MISSION LOGIC
The Paws & Peace Initiative is a **One Health** infrastructure project designed to stabilize the community ecosystem of Garden Hill. We are replacing the "Cold Path" of systemic neglect with a **Willing Sanctuary**—a managed network where human safety and animal health are engineered into balance.

### 🏗️ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (The Tech Layer)
*This project is managed by the **Steward Pilot** utilizing professional Information Systems (IS) logic.*

* **The Digital Archive:** A secure, encrypted database utilizing **Active Directory** principles to track animal population health, vaccination cycles, and lineage.
* **The Fear-to-Data Pipeline:** Mapping roaming pack locations to identify "Hotspots" of aggression, transforming community fear into actionable safety data.
* **Role-Based Access (RBAC):** Tiered permissions for "Sub-Pilots" (volunteers) to handle Front-End data entry while the core logic remains behind the Firewall.

### 🔍 THE "WINDIGO" ANALYSIS
Current community sensors indicate a critical failure in the local biological network:
* **Resource Depletion:** Pack cannibalism and predatory behavior.
* **Safety Gap:** Restricted human movement due to unmanaged animal aggression.
* **The Goal:** To move beyond temporary "patches" and build a permanent, local **Vessel of Care**.
