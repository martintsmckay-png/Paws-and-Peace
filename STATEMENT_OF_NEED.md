**Project Paws & Peace – Statement of Need**

**Background**
The Indigenous communities of Manitoba have long depended on healthy domestic and wild animal populations for cultural, economic, and ecological stability.  Recent disruptions in wildlife corridors, coupled with a lack of coordinated veterinary oversight, threaten both animal welfare and community resilience.

**Problem**
- 30 % decline in key wildlife species over the past 5 years.
- 45 % increase in zoonotic disease incidents due to unregulated animal movement.
- Lack of community‑led animal health programs; current services are fragmented and under‑funded.

**Objectives**
1. Establish a community‑driven One Health framework that integrates veterinary care, ecological monitoring, and traditional knowledge.
2. Build capacity for Indigenous animal keepers to conduct regular health checks, vaccination, and disease surveillance.
3. Create a sustainable funding model through grant partnerships and revenue‑sharing with local businesses.

**Approach**
- **Training & Capacity Building**: 12 workshops for community members, covering animal welfare, zoonosis prevention, and data management.
- **Field Infrastructure**: Mobile veterinary units equipped with diagnostics, cold‑chain storage, and digital reporting tools.
- **Data Integration**: A cloud‑based platform that aggregates health records, ecological data, and community feedback.
- **Outreach & Partnerships**: Collaboration with provincial health agencies, universities, and indigenous organizations.

**Expected Outcomes**
- 50 % reduction in zoonotic disease incidence within 3 years.
- 90 % of local animal owners trained in basic animal health practices.
- 1 new community animal health clinic operational by year 2.

**Budget Overview**
- Personnel: $120 000
- Equipment & Supplies: $80 000
- Training & Outreach: $30 000
- Operational Costs: $50 000
- Total: $280 000

**Conclusion**
Project Paws & Peace will create a resilient, culturally grounded animal health ecosystem that protects both wildlife and Indigenous livelihoods. Funding from your organization will enable us to safeguard community health and environmental integrity for generations.
